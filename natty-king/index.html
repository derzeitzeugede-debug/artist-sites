    Biography

    The story

    Natty King was born Kevin Christopher Roberts in 1977 and grew up in the countryside of St. Thomas, in the southeast of Jamaica. His heroes are The Wailers, Burning Spear and Luciano.

    In the early 2000s "Mr. Greedy" and "No Guns To Town" went to number one in Jamaica, on the charts and on the video charts. In 2003 he won Best New Entertainer at the IRAWMA awards in New York. He has played Sting, Rebel Salute, Reggae Sumfest and Rototom Sunsplash.

    His albums include No Guns To Town (2003), Born To Be Free (2010) and Rebellution (2022). His songs speak about faith, the environment and the youths.
