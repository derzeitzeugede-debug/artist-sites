Biography

Chuck Fenda was born Leshorn Dalton Whitehead on 15 June 1972 in Brooklyn, New York, and grew up between New York and Jamaica. Fans know him as "The Living Fire" and the "Poor People Defender".

He started out in the late 1990s with King Jammy and then joined the Fifth Element camp. The albums Better Days (2004), The Living Fire (2007), Fulfillment (2009) and Jah Elements (2013) followed. His song "All About Da Weed" is on the soundtrack of Grand Theft Auto IV, and "Warning" has more than six million plays on Spotify.

He calls his music conscious reggae. In summer 2026 he headlined the sold-out Lakesplash Festival in Switzerland and played the Turnpike Reggae Festival in Germany. New singles such as "Wah Gwaan Pon Di Rock" and "Take Time" keep coming.
