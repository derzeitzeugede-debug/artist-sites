    Biography

    The story

    Natural Black was born Mortimer Softley in Georgetown, Guyana, in 1975. In 1995 he moved to Jamaica to follow his dream of becoming a reggae singer. He worked as a welder and a coffee picker and recorded in his spare time.

    His breakthrough came in the early 2000s with producer Roger Grant and the singles "With Feeling" and "Bad Mind". Shows at Sting, Reggae Sumfest and Rebel Salute followed, and tours through France, Switzerland and Sweden.

    In 2006 "Far From Reality" for Don Corleon went to the top of the charts. His albums include Spiritual Food and World War. Today he is known far beyond Jamaica for his smooth voice and conscious lyrics.
