Everton Blender

Roots reggae with a smooth tenor from Clarendon, Jamaica. The voice behind "Lift Up Your Head" and "Ghetto People Song".

Back to website

Biography

Everton Blender was born Everton Dennis Williams on 21 November 1954 in Clarendon, Jamaica, and grew up on Maxfield Avenue in Kingston. In the late 1970s he won a talent contest at the Bohemia Club and sang on sound systems such as Destiny Outernational.

In the early 1990s Garnett Silk introduced him to the producer Richard Bell. "We No Jus' a Come" became a hit in Jamaica, and his first album Lift Up Your Head reached number four on the UK Black Echoes chart. Together with Garnett Silk and Luciano he stood for the return of conscious reggae in the 1990s, and with his own label Blend Dem Productions he also produced other artists.

He has never stopped: in 2024 he released the album Can't Take My Space, and new videos such as "Slavery Ship" and "Fools Illusion" keep coming.
