FAQ

Common questions

What does a website cost?

€1,129 per artist, or €850 per artist from three artists. Domain and hosting for the first year are included. From month 13 the care plan costs €19 per month.

Who owns the domain and the website?

The domain is registered in the name of the artist or the management. After full payment you get all access data and the right to use, change and host the site.

Do we have to prepare content?

No. The preview already has bio, discography and links. We only need approved photos, the booking email and your official profiles. We help with the rest.

What if we don't like the design?

Feedback is unlimited. We change texts, photos, colours and sections as often as needed until it fits.

Can we cancel the care plan?

Yes, every month without notice, by email. If you leave, we hand over the website files and the domain.

Who gets the shop commissions?

The artist, 100%. Where a shop or Apple Music pays referral commission, it goes to you, also for partner codes we register.

How fast can the site go live?

4 working days after we have your material and the down payment.

Can you also do merch?

Yes. The previews show design concepts. Real products follow once there is a merch agreement with the artist.
