Biography

Yami Bolo was born Rolando Ephraim McLean on 1 October 1970 in Kingston, Jamaica, and grew up in Kingston 13. He started singing in church and joined Sugar Minott's Youth Promotion crew as a teenager. His first singles came out in 1986.

In the late 1980s and early 1990s he recorded for Augustus Pablo's Rockers International, with songs like "Struggle In Babylon", followed by the albums He Who Knows It, Feels It (1991) and Up Life Street (1992) on Heartbeat. In 1994 his album Love Is Dangerous with the Japanese singer Miya sold 500,000 copies in Japan. He sings on "Still Searching" from Damian Marley's Halfway Tree, the 2002 Grammy winner for Best Reggae Album.

His songs speak about faith, Rastafari and justice, from "Blood Diamonds" (2008) to "Bloody Coltan" (2011). His own label Yam Euphony released the album Jah Love Will Rule the World in 2021, followed by Cool and Easy in 2024.
