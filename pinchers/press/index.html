Pinchers

Dancehall and reggae from Jamaica since the 1980s. The voice behind "Bandelero", "Agony" and "Send Another One Come".

Back to website

Biography

Pinchers was born Delroy Thompson on 19 November 1965 in Jamaica. He released his first album as a teenager on Blue Trac Records and spent a short time in the UK in 1985. His name follows a line of 1980s Jamaican artists named after tools, like Pliers, Screwdriver and Tenor Saw.

His first hits came in 1986 with "Borrow No Gun" for King Jammy, followed by "Abrakabra", "Eat Man" and "Jailhouse Hot". "Agony" made him famous, and the album Mass Out, produced by Philip "Fatis" Burrell with Sly & Robbie, came out the same year. In 1990 he released "Bandelero", his signature tune, later on the soundtrack of the film Shottas.

Hits like "Carpenter", "Send Another One Come" and "Venti Uno" with Bounty Killer followed. He still records and performs, known on stage for his elaborate outfits, with recent songs such as "Violence Cease", "Watch Your Friends" and "Old School" with King Kong and Burro Banton.
