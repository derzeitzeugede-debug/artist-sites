Norris Man

Roots reggae from Trenchtown, Kingston. The voice behind "Persistence" and the new album "Winner".

Back to website

Biography

Norris Man was born Christopher Campbell and grew up in Trenchtown and Arnett Gardens in Kingston, Jamaica, with Bob Marley as his hometown hero. He got his name from his love of karate and Chuck Norris. As a ten-year-old, older sound men lifted him onto a box and handed him the mic.

He recorded at Black Scorpio, Leggo and Celestial Sounds and, after turning to Rastafari in the mid-1990s, at Buju Banton's Cell Block studio. With the producer Richard "Bello" Bell he made the album Persistence (2000) with the anthems "Persistence", "Hold On To Your Faith" and "Bad Road". He performed "Persistence" at Sting 98.

Since then he has released more than 15 albums, from Ghetto Life (2012) to Deep Conversations (2022) and LIFE (2023). In 2024 he played Reggae Sumfest, in February 2026 he released the album Winner, and in November 2026 the single "Struggling" with Irie Ites and Naram.
