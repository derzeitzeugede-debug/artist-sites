Junior Kelly

Conscious reggae from Kingston, Jamaica. The voice behind "If Love So Nice" and the new album "Care About Me".

Back to website

Biography

Junior Kelly was born Keith Morgan on 23 September 1969 in Kingston, Jamaica. He is the younger brother of the deejay Jim Kelly and made his first recording in 1985.

His breakthrough came in 1999 with "If Love So Nice", which became a hit in Jamaica and around the world. The albums Rise (2000), Love So Nice (2001) with the hit "Boom Draw", Smile (2003) and Tough Life (2005) followed. Red Pond (2010) features Ras Shiloh, Lukie D and Queen Ifrica, and Urban Poet (2015) took him on tour through Europe and the Caribbean.

He is still writing and recording: "Protection" (2024), "Everything" (2025) and "Perseverance" with Torch (2026) came out as videos, and in 2026 he released his new album Care About Me.
