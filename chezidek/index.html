    Biography

    The story

    Chezidek was born Desbert Johnson in 1973 in St. Ann, Jamaica. He sang at school concerts and in the St. Ann's Bay Marching Band, and later performed on local sound systems as Chilla Rinch.

    In Kingston he linked up with the producer Philip "Fatis" Burrell, and in 2002 his first album Harvest Time came out on VP Records. "Leave De Trees" went into the BBC dancehall top 10, and "Call Pon Dem" from the album Inna Di Road (2007) is part of the Grand Theft Auto IV soundtrack. His video "All My Life" has more than 40 million views.

    He has played Rebel Salute, Sting and stages across Europe and Africa. In 2026 he released his new album Marijuana Prayer.
