GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

3. Fonts

Fonts are loaded locally from this server. No connection to Google Fonts is made.

4. Embedded third-party content

Links to shops and streaming services only take you to their sites when you click them. The Spotify player (Spotify AB, Sweden), YouTube videos further down the page and tour dates from Bandsintown (Bandsintown Group, USA) are only loaded when you click the respective button (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

5. Newsletter

For the newsletter sign-up we process your email address after confirmation by double opt-in (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email. No data is sent in the preview.

6. Booking form

Details from the form (name, email, message, date/place) are processed solely to answer your enquiry and are forwarded to the management (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Sending service: [e.g. Formspree Inc., USA]. No data is sent in the preview.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
