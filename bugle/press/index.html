Biography

Bugle was born Roy Thompson in Portland, Jamaica. He started writing songs in primary school and got his first real chance in 2000, when he began writing for and touring with Elephant Man.

After five years on the road, from the Caribbean to the USA, Canada and Japan, he went solo. With the production team Daseca he found his own sound on songs like "Journeys", "Anointed" and "Nuh Compatible".

Today Bugle is one of the most respected voices in modern Jamaican music, with more than 500 releases. His single "Thank You Lord" with Buju Banton and Damian "Jr. Gong" Marley has been watched more than 14 million times.
