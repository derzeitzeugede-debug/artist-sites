Glen Washington

Soulful roots and lovers rock from May Pen, Jamaica. A voice that has carried reggae for five decades.

Biography

Glen Washington, born Glenroy Washington in 1957, grew up in May Pen, Jamaica. He started out as a drummer and singer and cut his first record, "Rockers Nu Crackers", in the late 1970s.

For years he worked mostly behind the scenes as a musician and producer. In 1998 "Kindness For Weakness" brought him back to the charts and started one of the most consistent careers in roots reggae.

Since then he has released more than 20 albums for Studio One, VP Records, Joe Fraser, Zion High Productions, Love Injection and Tad's Record. His husky, soulful voice moves easily between conscious roots and lovers rock. His latest album is Just Giving Thanks (2025).
