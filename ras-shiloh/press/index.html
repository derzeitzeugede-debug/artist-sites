Biography

Ras Shiloh was born Thomas Williams in Brooklyn, New York, in 1975. He grew up with the music of the Jamaican diaspora and found his calling in roots reggae and the Rastafari faith.

His first album Chants came out in 1997. From Rasta To You (2002) and Coming Home (2007) on VP Records and Only King Selassie (2007) on Greensleeves made his name known worldwide. For more than 25 years his clear tenor has carried a message of faith, unity and love.

He has recorded for Penthouse, Maximum Sound, Firehouse Crew and No Doubt, and he still releases new music, most recently the single "Think" (2025).
