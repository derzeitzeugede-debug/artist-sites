Biography

The story

Jah Mason, born Andre Johnson in Manchester Parish, Jamaica, has been recording since 1991, when his first single came out under the name Perry Mason. Known to many as Fire Mason, he moves easily between singing and deejaying.

His catalogue runs to nearly twenty studio albums, among them Keep Your Joy and Working So Hard (2002), Wheat and Tears (2006, Greensleeves), Princess Gone... The Saga Bed (2006, VP) and Love & Wisdom (2015). He also cut The Pow Pow Trilogy (2008) together with Anthony B and Turbulence.

From early Jah Cure combinations on Henfield to his 2026 seven inch on Five Disciples Crew Records, his music has been pressed on vinyl by labels across Jamaica and Europe.
