Biography

The story

Lutan Fyah, born Anthony Martin in Spanish Town, Jamaica, studied architecture and played football for Constant Spring before music took over. He started recording in 1999 and has released a steady stream of roots reggae ever since.

His albums include Dem No Know Demself (2004), Time & Place (2005), Phantom War (2006, Greensleeves), Healthy Lifestyle (2006, VP), Life of a King (2013), Longest Liva (2019) and Strength & Resilience (2025, I Grade Records).

On vinyl he has voiced riddims for Xterminator, Silly Walks, Irie Ites, Oneness and many more, alongside artists such as Luciano, Anthony B, Turbulence and Morgan Heritage.
