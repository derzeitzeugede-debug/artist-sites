Turbulence

Jamaican singjay between conscious reggae and dancehall. The voice behind "Notorious" and "Celebration of Life".

Biography

Turbulence was born Sheldon Campbell on 11 January 1980 in Jamaica. In 1999 the producer Philip "Fatis" Burrell discovered him, and his first album Rising followed in 2001.

His breakthrough came with "Notorious" on the Scallawah riddim. In 2005 the song went to number one on reggae charts in Jamaica, New York, London, Toronto and Japan. Since then he has released more than 20 albums, from Songs of Solomon and Notorious to Celebration of Life (2012) and The Remedy (2021). He also appears in the award-winning documentary Rise Up (2007).

His music comes from old-school roots, inspired by Beres Hammond, Cocoa Tea, Sizzla and Luciano. In 2025 he toured Africa, the Bahamas and Guadeloupe, and in 2026 he released new singles such as "Rasta Nah Surrender".
